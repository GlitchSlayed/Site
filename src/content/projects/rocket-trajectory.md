## The question

What can a simple vertical flight model reliably tell us?

## Proposed approach

Begin with gravity-only verification, add motor thrust and mass depletion, then include signed drag. Check time-step convergence and compare with a suitable independent tool.

## Planned deliverables

A documented solver, verification cases, altitude and velocity plots, and sensitivity studies.

## What would count as progress?

A well-defined baseline, traceable inputs, independent verification where possible, and a clear explanation of what the model can and cannot establish.
