## The question

Which configuration best meets a specified mission?

## Proposed approach

Define one mission and two consistent baseline configurations. Estimate aerodynamic performance, record mass assumptions, and study how uncertainty changes the comparison.

## Planned deliverables

A transparent notebook, comparison plots, a parameter table, and a written discussion of limitations.

## What would count as progress?

A well-defined baseline, traceable inputs, independent verification where possible, and a clear explanation of what the model can and cannot establish.
